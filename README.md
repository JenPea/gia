# Gia

An interactive, map-style platform for understanding and managing complex organizations, particularly creative studios and production companies.

Zoom out to see the whole organization. Zoom in to reach a department, a team, a budget line, a relationship, a risk, or an operational detail. One continuous surface instead of a stack of separate spreadsheets, org charts, and slide decks.

## The idea

Most organizations are described in disconnected documents: an org chart here, a budget there, a risk register somewhere else. None of them show how the pieces relate. Gia puts everything on a single zoomable canvas, so you can move between the big picture and the detail without switching tools or losing context.

## What you can see at each level

- **Organization:** departments, major projects, overall health, total budget
- **Department:** teams, leads, active projects, spend against plan
- **Team:** people, roles, workload, dependencies on other teams
- **Detail:** budget lines, risks, contracts, deadlines, notes

Relationships (who depends on whom, which projects share resources, where budget flows) are drawn on the map rather than buried in tables.

## Early scope (to be confirmed)

- A nested, zoomable canvas as the core interface
- Departments, teams, projects, and people as the basic building blocks
- Budgets and risks attached to any node
- Relationship lines between nodes
- Layers to switch what the map emphasizes (budget, risk, people, schedule)
